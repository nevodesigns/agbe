# Source registry

`registry.json` lists every source family with its licence, tier and verification status. Nothing is used for training unless its tier is A.

- `cgspace_tier_a.jsonl` is generated by `tools/cgspace_harvest.py`. It holds metadata only (title, authors, licence, abstract, handle). Full text is a later step.
- `cgspace_summary.json` records what was harvested and how many NC and SA items were seen and excluded.

Rules:
1. An item with no stated licence is not Tier A, even if the publisher's policy says "free to reuse".
2. NC and SA items are never emitted by the harvester and never trained on.
3. Every fact later extracted keeps the handle, the page and the licence of its source.
4. "to_verify" means I have not confirmed it yet. Do not rely on it.
