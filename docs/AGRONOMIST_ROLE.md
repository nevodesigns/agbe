# Agronomist reviewer: who and what

AGBE needs one or two domain experts to review the fact base and the gold questions. The role is part time, remote and can start at a few hours a month.

## Must have

- A degree or equivalent practice in crop science, agronomy, plant protection, soil science or animal science (livestock and poultry need their own reviewer if the first is a crop specialist).
- Working knowledge of Nigerian and West African smallholder systems: the crops, pests, diseases, seasons and inputs a farmer there actually uses.
- Hands-on field or extension experience, not only research. They should know what a farmer can afford and has access to.
- Comfort saying "this answer is wrong and here is why" in writing, with a source.

## Strongly preferred

- Plant protection or integrated pest management background. Pesticide and dose questions are where harm happens.
- Familiarity with extension materials from IITA, NAERLS, state ADPs or similar, and how local names map to scientific ones.
- Fluency in a local language (Hausa, Yoruba, Igbo or Pidgin) for the language work later.
- Willingness to be named and credited, or to review anonymously.

## What they would do

1. Audit a random sample of extracted claims from the fact base: correct, wrong, too absolute, or missing a condition.
2. Write or approve gold questions with the expected claims, so the model is tested against expert truth.
3. Review answers the model gives on the gold set and mark unsafe ones.
4. Advise on which advice must always defer to a human (doses, livestock treatment, human health).

## What they would not need to do

Write code, train anything or use a terminal. Everything is delivered as spreadsheets or short documents.

## Where to look

University departments of crop science and animal science, the Federal and State ADP extension staff, IITA and NAERLS, the Agricultural Extension Society of Nigeria, and the mentors from the ADTC programme.
