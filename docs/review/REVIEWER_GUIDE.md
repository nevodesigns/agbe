# Human review of AGBE answers

Why: the guard and the scorer share one claim checker, so their numbers are not independent. A person who knows Nigerian smallholder farming is the only real check.

Sheet: `answer-review-sheet-2026-10-23.csv` (50 answers from the held-out set, 30 grounded and 20 where the sources do not answer; run2 Qwen3 1.7B Q4 with the short prompt). The reviewer marks each answer Yes, Partly or No, notes anything dangerous or misleading, and writes corrected advice.

Reviewer qualities: final-year or postgraduate student in crop production, soil science, agronomy or agricultural extension; hands-on with smallholder cassava, maize, yam, rice or cowpea; fluent in Yoruba and Pidgin; careful and willing to mark answers wrong; 4 to 6 hours available; credited by name or kept anonymous as they prefer.

Faculty advisor qualities: crop science, soil fertility, plant protection or extension; published on West African smallholder farming; able to sign off the student's marks and judge whether the fact base is safe for farmers.

Outreach: a two-post thread on X to FUNAAB asking to be connected, sent after 23 October 2026. The result quoted is top 10 finalist among 291 entries, not a win.

What to do with the marks: count Yes, Partly and No; fix or remove claims reviewers mark wrong; report the human pass rate beside the automatic one.
