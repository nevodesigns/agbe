## 1. IITA

**To:** iita@cgiar.org
**Cc:** e.ezomo@cgiar.org
**Subject:** Permission request: using IITA publications to ground AGBE, an offline farm advisor (ADTC 2026 top 10 finalist)

Dear Elsie Ezomo and the IITA Knowledge Center team,

My name is Nwokolo Victor Oluebubechukwu, a solo builder in Lagos, Nigeria. I built AGBE, an offline agricultural advisor for West and Central African smallholders. It is a small language model, 814 MB, that runs on an ordinary laptop with no internet. AGBE was one of 10 finalists, chosen from 291 submissions, in the Africa Deep Tech Challenge 2026.

I am now building the next version, and I want it grounded in verified sources instead of my own summaries. IITA's work on cassava, maize, yam, cowpea, plantain, striga, aflatoxin and the pests and diseases around them is the best source for West Africa, and I want to use it properly.

Where I stand today: I use only CGSpace items that state an open licence. I have catalogued 7,890 such items, 2,962 of them from IITA, and I read each item's licence from CGSpace again before I use it. As I understand IITA's open-access guidance, the default licence for IITA publications is CC BY 4.0, and I would like to confirm that this is how you want those items used. This request covers that, and the material beyond it.

What I would do with IITA material:
- Extract individual facts (a spacing, a planting window, how to tell two pests apart) and write each one in my own words.
- Attach the source, page and licence to every fact, and let the model cite the publication it is answering from.
- Use those facts to train and test a small model.
- Never reproduce your text or figures, never present AGBE's answers as IITA's, and credit IITA on the website, the model card and in answers that cite it.

What I am asking:
1. Confirmation that IITA publications on CGSpace stated as CC BY 4.0 may be used this way, including by a model that partner platforms may later offer to farmers.
2. Written permission for IITA publications that carry no stated licence, on the same terms, or a statement of the conditions you would want.
3. A pointer to an agronomist or extension specialist at IITA who could review the fact base. A few hours of expert review would change its quality more than anything else I can do.

I will send you the evaluation results and the list of every fact drawn from IITA material, so you can check it. Any correction you make goes straight into the next version.

You can see the work at https://agbe-omega.vercel.app and https://github.com/nevodesigns/agbe. I would be glad to answer questions or join a short call.

Thank you for your time, and for the research that makes this possible.

Kind regards,

## Before you send

- Send each email separately, with its own subject line.
- Check that the Cc addresses do not bounce. If e.ezomo@cgiar.org bounces, drop it and use the IITA contact form.
- Follow up once after two weeks. If there is still no answer, treat that material as Tier C and do not use it.
- Keep every reply. A permission only counts if it is in writing.
- If an organisation names specific documents, record them in `data/sources/registry.json` with the date and the reply.
