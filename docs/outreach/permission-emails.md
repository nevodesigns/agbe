## Main email (use for IITA, then adapt)

**Subject:** Permission request: using [IITA] publications to ground AGBE, an offline farm advisor (ADTC 2026 top 10 finalist)

Dear [Name or Publications and Library team],

My name is Nwokolo Victor Oluebubechukwu, a solo builder in Lagos, Nigeria. I built AGBE, an offline agricultural advisor for West and Central African smallholders. It is a small language model, 814 MB, that runs on an ordinary laptop with no internet. AGBE was one of 10 finalists, chosen from 291 submissions, in the Africa Deep Tech Challenge 2026.

I am now building the next version, and I want it grounded in verified sources instead of my own summaries. Your publications on [cassava, maize, yam, cowpea, striga, aflatoxin and related pests and diseases] are the best source for West Africa, and I would like to use them properly, with permission.

What I would do with them:
- Extract individual facts (a spacing, a planting window, how to tell two pests apart) and write each in my own words.
- Attach the source, page and licence to every fact, and let the model cite the publication it is answering from.
- Use those facts to train and test a small model.
- Never reproduce your text or figures, never present AGBE's answers as yours, and credit [IITA] on the website, the model card and in answers that cite it.

What I am asking:
1. Written permission, or a licence, for these uses for [the publications listed below / IITA publications on the topics above]. AGBE may later be offered through partner platforms, so I am asking for terms that cover that, and I am open to any conditions you want to set.
2. If you can, a pointer to an agronomist or extension specialist who could review the fact base. A few hours of expert review would change its quality.

I will send you the evaluation results and the list of every fact drawn from your material, so you can check it. Any correction you make goes straight into the next version. I already use only items that state an open licence such as CC BY, so this request is for the material beyond that.

You can see the work here: https://agbe-omega.vercel.app and https://github.com/nevodesigns/agbe. I am glad to answer questions or join a short call.

Thank you for your time, and for the research that makes this possible.

Kind regards,
Nwokolo Victor Oluebubechukwu

## Before you send

- One email per organisation, each with its own subject line.
- If you list specific publications, use the title, year and CGSpace handle (for IITA) or document number (for FAO).
- Follow up once after two weeks. If there is no answer, treat the material as Tier C and do not use it.
- Keep every reply. A permission only counts if it is in writing.
